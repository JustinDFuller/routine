# Dogfooding Checklist

Use this checklist before relying on the MVP on a simulator or personal iPhone.

- Install and launch the app from a clean build.
- Confirm the launcher icon is visible and recognizable on the Home Screen.
- Confirm the launcher icon is visible in the App Library.
- Verify starter data appears on first launch.
- Force-quit and relaunch the app, then confirm the existing data persists.
- Complete a routine from the dashboard and confirm the card updates immediately.
- Attempt a duplicate same-day completion and confirm it behaves as a no-op.
- Undo a same-day completion and confirm the dashboard returns to the prior state.
- Open routine history and remove a historical completion.
- Confirm the history screen and dashboard reflect the correction after returning.
- Add a new routine.
- Edit an existing routine.
- Delete a routine and confirm its history is removed.
- Add a new group.
- Rename a group.
- Reorder routines within a group.
- Move a routine into a different group.
- Reorder groups.
- Verify the app remains usable in both dark mode and light mode.
- Verify the app remains usable with larger Dynamic Type sizes.
- Verify VoiceOver can reach the primary dashboard, history, and management flows.
- Verify Reduce Motion does not introduce broken transitions or blocked flows.
- Verify the primary dashboard interactions remain comfortable for one-handed use on iPhone.
